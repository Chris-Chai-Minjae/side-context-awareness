## Observed language

1. A single narrow Settings column sits under a 64px top navigation bar.
2. White canvas, fine gray rules, modest corner radii, and restrained blue controls establish the light theme.
3. Headings are direct, bold system UI; secondary explanation uses the muted ink token.
4. Day uses an article and a distinct right evidence column; source links are underlined.
5. Mobile stacks settings row controls below their explanations and retains the top navigation.
