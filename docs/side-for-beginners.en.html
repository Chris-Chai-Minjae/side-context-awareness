Side · A beginner's guide

Remember what you did today.
Understand and use Side.

A plain-language guide to how Side records activity in browsers and Mac apps, makes optional summaries, and helps your agent find the source again. It also covers installation and real questions you can ask.

0First, what is Side?

It is a personal record for the question, “Where did I see that yesterday?”

Imagine that yesterday you read a page in your browser, wrote a sentence in a notes app, and checked something in another app. Days later, you cannot remember the exact title or address. You can ask an agent connected to Side to find it. Side records text and activity from allowed windows on your Mac so you can find them with their sources. If you configure a summary provider and consent to sending evidence, it can also make daily summaries.

Side can observe the active window of Mac apps as well as browser tabs. This does not mean it remembers everything on your Mac. It needs permission to read a window, and it does not observe excluded apps or websites or password fields. When accessible text is unavailable, optional screen text recognition can help.

A WORD TO KNOW
Capture (recording activity)
Side collects clues from the window you are using, such as its title, address, readable text, and a sentence after you finish typing it. It does not mean continuously saving a video of your screen.
A WORD TO KNOW
Summary (a shorter account)
A written account of what the collected clues suggest you did. Side can make summaries for 10-minute windows and longer groups, then arrange them on a daily page.

1Side in one sentence

Think of a personal notebook paired with a library catalog.

THE MAIN IDEA

Side notes clues from your day
and helps you find their sources later.

Without writing every note yourself, you can leave clues about what you saw in allowed apps. When you ask a connected agent, it can find a related record and tell you when you saw it, its title, and any source you can check. It can also find a summary if you enabled summaries and one was actually made.

Picture an assistant who takes notes and a librarian who knows where to find them. The assistant may miss a screen, and a summary may be wrong, so reopen the source before relying on an important fact.

2How activity becomes a memory

The four steps are observe, store locally, summarize, and recall.

Side starts with the window you are currently using. Accessibility permission lets it read available text and window information. Input Monitoring helps it notice interactions and when a typed sentence is complete. If a screen has no readable text, and you allow it, OCR (screen text recognition) extracts text from the screen. Side does not keep the image as a record.

Raw records go into an encrypted local ledger (a time-ordered log). Side masks known password, token, and card-number patterns first, but rules cannot catch every sensitive value. It creates daily summary pages and a search index as separate files. Raw captures are kept for 14 days by default; summaries remain separately until you delete them.

A DRAWER AND INDEX CARDS

Think of a raw record as a receipt in a locked drawer. A summary is like a diary entry about your day. The search index is a set of cards that helps you find the right part quickly.

Real records are less complete than receipts: an app with unreadable text, a private screen, or an exclusion rule can leave a gap.

3How does Side find things?

A time, a word, or a topic can be enough to start a search.

Tell Side roughly when you saw something and any words you remember. It can search related captures or browser visits. For example, ask for “the restaurant booking page I saw yesterday.” If it finds only a browser visit, you can see the visit time, title, and address, but not the page's full text.

If you do not remember the exact wording, try a topic such as “the refund policy I looked at last week.” This can help when daily summaries were actually created. Side cannot find a screen it did not record because of permissions or settings. Check the original source again when the answer matters.

More detail · Search tools and source IDs
WHEN YOU REMEMBER A CLUE

history_search — Find words and times

It looks for words in recorded app names, page titles, addresses, and typed sentences. Recent activity gets a boost. Use it when you remember a time or phrase. It searches Side captures e:, summaries s:, and browser visits h:; it does not use embedding vectors. An h: result has a visit time, title, and address, but no page body.

WHEN THE WORDS ARE FUZZY

memory_search — Find words and meaning

It searches daily summary pages using both words and embeddings (numbers representing a sentence's meaning). A record might say “screen text recognition” while your question says “OCR problem”; this search may still find it. Side calculates embeddings locally using the MiniLM model bundled with the app.

WHEN YOU NEED THE SOURCE

history_read — Open a source ID

Copy an e: or s: ID from a search result to read the original capture or summary. You cannot open an h: ID with this tool; you only get its title and address in the search result. A raw capture may be unavailable after its retention period, though a related summary may remain.

WHAT IS A VECTOR?
Vector search (finding text with similar meaning)
It turns sentences into numerical coordinates and looks for nearby ones, like a library recommending a book on a similar topic even when its title differs. Side uses this for summarized daily pages, not for every raw event.

4How do I ask an agent?

Keep Side running and ask a connected agent in everyday language.

Side provides MCP (a way to connect AI agents to tools) so an agent can search your records. Register Side's tools separately in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok Build, Aside, or another client. Installing Side alone does not connect them, and Side.app must be running when you ask.

You can also register Side in Aside's Settings → MCP → Add server screen, with name side, command /Applications/Side.app/Contents/Resources/side, and argument mcp. Aside then calls the Side tools on the same Mac. This does not automatically sync records to another device. If you installed Side elsewhere, use that app's actual executable path.

EXAMPLE 1 · A PAGE FROM YESTERDAY

I cannot remember the restaurant's name

“Find the restaurant booking page I saw yesterday evening. Tell me when I visited, the page title, and its address.”

The time and a few remembered words help narrow the choices. A browser-visit-only result can give you the visit time, title, and address, but no page text.

EXAMPLE 2 · I ONLY REMEMBER THE TOPIC

I want to check the refund policy again

“Find the refund policy page I read last week. If you have the original text or a source for the free-cancellation period, show me.”

You can give a topic and an approximate date without the exact title. Content is available only if a readable capture or a created summary exists. If you get only a title and address, check the policy on that page yourself.

EXAMPLE 3 · ASKING IN ASIDE

I want to find my travel plans

“Search Side for the travel itinerary page I looked at last week. Tell me when I saw it, the page title, and anything you can verify from the record.”

This works in Aside when Side's tools are registered there and Side.app is running. Results are limited to records Side still has. Summaries appear only if you enabled them and they were actually created.

Connect Side tools · once after installation
claude mcp add --scope user side -- "/Applications/Side.app/Contents/Resources/side" mcpConnect at user scope in Claude Code
codex mcp add side -- "/Applications/Side.app/Contents/Resources/side" mcpConnect in Codex
grok mcp add --scope user side -- "/Applications/Side.app/Contents/Resources/side" mcpLet Grok Build search Side records
Copy a command into your terminal while Side is running. Registering Grok Build as a search client is separate from choosing a summary provider; an actual Grok Build tool call has not yet been verified. Cursor and Aside setup screens are described in the agent connection guide.

5Install and set up Side

Build the source on your Mac, then put the app in Applications.

Side is currently distributed as source; there is no prebuilt app to download and open. Clone the source from GitHub, then run the commands below in a terminal. You need macOS 14 or later, Bun, Xcode Command Line Tools, and a SQLite dylib with FTS5 and extension loading. The build script looks for Homebrew's SQLite; if your copy is elsewhere, set SIDE_SQLITE_LIBRARY to its path. The build also bundles the MiniLM model, so it may need internet access the first time.

Local build · copy each line in order
git clone https://github.com/Chris-Chai-Minjae/side-context-awareness.gitDownload Side's source from GitHub
cd side-context-awarenessEnter the downloaded folder
bun install --frozen-lockfileInstall the required packages
bun run buildBuild the Mac app
ditto apps/side-mac/.build/release/Side.app /Applications/Side.appCopy the app into Applications
open /Applications/Side.appStart the menu bar app
Copy one line at a time. If Side is already running, quit it before copying a new build over the app.

On first launch, Side guides you through Accessibility and Input Monitoring permissions. Screen Recording is needed for OCR but is optional. For summaries, you can enter a provider API key, which Side stores in Keychain. If Claude Code is installed and logged in on your Mac, you can instead choose its existing login as a summary-provider option. After configuring a provider, you must still turn on “Send evidence to this provider” in settings before Side uses real activity to make summaries.

You choose your summary providers. For example, you can try MiMo 2.6 Pro first and set MiniMax M3 as the next provider. Even with a working provider connection, a particular summary can fail because of a model error or an invalid response format. If the history screen looks empty, check whether the 10-minute window has ended and whether summaries are ready.

AFTER REBUILDING THE APP
macOS permissions may still point to the old app

Local builds use ad hoc signing. If Side cannot access a permission after replacement, remove the old Side entry in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then add /Applications/Side.app again. Check the app path before repeatedly toggling the same permission.

DIAGNOSIS
Check setup in one step

Run "/Applications/Side.app/Contents/Resources/side" doctor in a terminal to check Keychain, permissions, SQLite, the vector-search extension, the model cache, and provider connections. The provider check uses a synthetic sentence rather than your real activity.

6Retention, deletion, and privacy

You choose what Side remembers and for how long.

From the menu bar, pause capture for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, one hour, or until you resume it yourself. Add apps and websites you do not want observed to the exclusions list in settings. Choose a raw-capture retention period of 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days; the default is 14 days. In settings, you can delete the last 10 minutes, the last hour, today's records, or all Side history.

Side stores its data on this Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Side/. It masks known sensitive patterns before saving and encrypts sensitive columns of raw records, but no pattern list catches every secret. Summaries and daily pages remain readable text in local files. Deleting all Side history removes its raw records, summaries, and indexes and rotates the encryption key. Your browser's own history remains, and model API keys remain separately in macOS Keychain.

WHEN CONNECTING AN EXTERNAL MODEL
Check what you allow Side to send

Turning on “Send evidence to this provider” sends masked 10-minute activity briefings and six-hour rollup briefings to the displayed model-provider address. A connected agent may also pass Side search results to its own model. Side cannot control each service's retention policy.

WHAT YOU CAN CONTROL
Pause, exclude, and delete

Pause capture in the menu bar when you need a break. Use app and website exclusions to keep particular places out of future captures. Use Delete History in settings to remove records Side already made. Turning off Context Awareness does not immediately erase past records. To remove browser visit history, delete it separately in your browser.

YOUR FIRST TRY

Check the flow with one short activity

  1. Check permissions and status. Open Side and run "/Applications/Side.app/Contents/Resources/side" doctor to see whether the required pieces are ready.
  2. Read one memorable document. Spend a few minutes on a public page with a distinctive title. You do not need secret material for your first test.
  3. Ask an agent for the source. Try “Find the page I just read. Tell me when I saw it, its title, and any source I can check.” A browser-visit-only result gives you a title and address. If you enabled summaries, you may find one after it has been created.
